# ORAE Presents Dhamaka '26

High-converting, mobile-first luxury landing page for **ORAE Presents Dhamaka '26** — Farm & Bonfire Party in NCR.

## Event Details
- **Date & Time:** Friday, 6 November 2026 • 4:00 PM to 11:00 PM
- **Venue:** Nature Farm Stay with Lawn and Bonfire, near Dwarka Expressway
- **Dress Code:** Ethnic, vibrant, colourful and festive

## Features
- **Dynamic 4K Video Wallpaper:** Cinematic auto-chaining video background (`video1.mp4` teaser & `video2.mp4` full experience).
- **Smart Dark Glassmorphism Navbar:** Auto-hides on scroll down, reveals on scroll up, with glowing center seal and direct CTA.
- **Dynamic Ticket Pricing Calculator:** Handles Stag (Alcoholic / Non-Alcoholic) and Couple (Alcoholic / Non-Alcoholic) passes with pass count calculator.
- **Live UPI QR & Screenshot Validation:** Integrated payment desk with instant UPI copying and receipt image preview.
- **Celebration Confetti Modal:** Instant booking reference and guest confirmation modal.
- **Fully Responsive & Clean Code:** Built with modern HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.
